# Harris Dynamics Public Lab

Harris Dynamics is an independent robotics and personal-manufacturing lab founded by **Timmy Harris Jr.**

This repository is the public record of selected, non-confidential work. It shows the lab's engineering process without publishing protected product files or private information.

## Mission

Build character-driven, repairable robots and useful machines through disciplined prototyping, testing and documentation.

## Lead Development Program

The first platform proof centers on:

1. **Ninja Bot Battle Ready Pair** — two compact robot characters that share hardware while retaining individual identity and behavior.
2. **HD-USA-1S universal armor interface** — a printable receiver, panel and latch system designed for repeatable fit and controlled release.
3. **Safe prototype sequence** — fit coupons first, followed by protected power, emergency stop, raised-wheel drive, low-speed movement and controlled interaction.

## Evidence Rules

- Concept art is not hardware evidence.
- Target prices, runtimes and performance remain targets until measured.
- Failed tests are documented rather than hidden.
- Private credentials and confidential design details are never published.
- Battery, movement and impact tests use staged safety gates.

## Community Work

Harris Dynamics plans to release separate community-safe projects such as educational mechanisms, collectibles, games, organizers and useful household objects under clearly stated licenses. These releases will not expose protected robot technology.
